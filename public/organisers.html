Wingman

Every conference already has an app nobody opens.

Wingman is the "who should I meet" layer for your event: a closed room where everyone was admitted the same way, and the people worth meeting find each other before the coffee break ends.

Everyone here was on the organiser's list.

That one sentence is the product. A circle is worth exactly what its admission is worth, so admission is proved, never typed: your attendee list becomes salted hashes on the organiser's device, and each delegate proves the address you have for them before they are in. Nobody can claim their way into your room.

What your delegates get

Verified people only, women only, your circle only — in both directions. BankID for those who want the strongest room.

One sentence, not a profile

A first name and what they are working on. Nothing else until both say yes.

Safety on every surface

Hide, report and mute anywhere a person appears. A friend can watch over a meet with a link that dies when it ends.

The room after the room

A shared flight home, a layover in the same terminal, the same city next month. The event is where it starts.

Why now

National eID is ordinary in the Nordics, the EU Digital Identity Wallet lands in every member state by the end of 2026, and your delegates already carry a verified identity in their pocket. A room where everyone has proved who they are used to need a registration desk. Now it needs a link.

What you get

A report a sponsor can read: how many joined, how many met through the circle — bucketed on purpose, because an exact figure on a small circle is a roster. Free while in beta; per-event pricing for organisers comes with the server. Travellers never pay, and there are no ads.
