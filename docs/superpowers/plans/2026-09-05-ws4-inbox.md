# WS4 — Inbox: safety surfaces first, then every conversation in one list

> Scoped in `2026-09-05-wingman-v4.md` § WS4. Cites: W2–W5 W7 W8 C6 V6 J1 J5 T8.

Group chat cannot ship before block, report and mute exist (App Store 1.2; W2 is S1), so 4a lands first and 4b builds on it.

## 4a · Safety

1. **Domain** — `domain/safety.ts`: `SafetyReport { id, personId, channelId?, messageId?, reason: SafetyFlag | 'other', note?, at }`. `VerificationRecord.mocked?: boolean`.
2. **Store** (v4) — `reports`, `muted: ChannelId[]`, `guardian: GuardianSession | null`, `ratings: Rating[]`; actions `reportPerson`, `reportMessage`, `unblockPerson`, `muteChannel`, `unmuteChannel`, `armGuardian`, `checkOut`, `escalate`, `rate`.
3. **Primitives** — `Menu` (a "⋯" button and a small popover of actions; Escape and outside-click close).
4. **`screens/safety/`** — `PersonMenu` (Hide from me · Report · Mute in channels), `ReportSheet`, `AcceptSheet` (what level 2 reveals, from the ladder), `GuardianSheet` + status chip + "I'm safe", `AfterMeet`.
5. **Behaviour** — the other person's terminal only after their first stage update; `selectors/requests.ts` applies `expireIfDue` on read and shows "Expires in N h"; `redact.ts` omits stamps whose record is `mocked`; `useVerify.finish` stamps `mocked` when `isMocked`.
6. **You → Safety** — "Right now N people can see your card" (bucketed, `whoCanSeeMe`), Preview as a stranger (`previewAs`), blocked list with Unblock, reports sent.

## 4b · One inbox

7. **Domain** — `domain/channel.ts`: `ChannelKind`, `Channel`, `Message`, `MessageBody` (text · stage · system). `MeetMessage` stays exported for the migration only.
8. **Migration v3 → v4** — every `MeetMessage` becomes a `Message` in a `meet:<requestId>` channel; `readAt: Record<ChannelId, ISODateTime>` is new and persisted (a fact about the reader).
9. **Seed** — `data/seed/channels.ts`: INSEAD General (the demo person is a member) with six messages and an organiser pin; a group "Singapore this week" of three INSEAD members; Grid Week General for anyone who joins. `data/seed/replies.ts`: one scripted reply per channel kind, deterministic, appended by `post` so the demo feels alive without pretending to be a server.
10. **Selectors** — `selectors/inbox.ts`: rows = pending incoming (strip) + outgoing requests + channels (meet for accepted requests, circle General for each membership, groups), sorted needs-you first then last activity; unread by `readAt`; muted rows never sort to the top. `selectors/channel.ts`: one channel's messages, participants, and for a meet channel the presence (terminal after first update).
11. **`screens/inbox/`** — `InboxScreen` (`#/inbox`; one list; filter row All · Meets · Circles · Groups; the requests strip), `ChannelScreen` (`#/inbox/:channelId`; meet keeps the room, circle shows the pin, group is the circle layout without it), `NewGroupSheet`. `requests/` and `meet/` are deleted; `#/requests` and `#/meet/:id` stay as aliases. The tab is **Inbox**; its dot counts unread + pending.
12. **Minimalism rules** — one list, one filter row, no section headers inside the list, no timestamps longer than `HH:MM` / `Mon` / `12 Sep`, avatars ≤ 40 px, ≤ 2 lines per row, nothing on a row that is not tappable.
13. **Tests** — `channel.migrate.test.ts`, `inbox.selector.test.ts`, `redact` mocked-stamp omission, `ChannelScreen.uitest.tsx` (no terminal before an update; hide from the header removes the row), `e2e/inbox.spec.ts`.
