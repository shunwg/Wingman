## The two blockers

**Round 4 — listings ignored hides and audiences (pre-existing).** `useCircleMembers` and `eventBoards` asked only "did they choose `show_badge`". A person you hid stayed on the event you were both at, and a verified-only member was listed, with photo and bio, to an unverified stranger. Both now go through `listedMembers` (`src/state/selectors/circles.ts`). It applies `admitsListing` (`src/privacy/resolve.ts`), which runs the same identity rules a car's door uses: blocks either way, gender, assurance, stamps, circle, intent. Since round 5 the list is also members-only, and honours "When not travelling".

**Round 5 — a membership oracle (v9, introduced by round 4's fix).** Round 4 added a notice to circle rooms, "Someone you hid is in this circle", computed from the circle's member list. So hiding a person told you whether they belonged, even someone who had chosen never to be listed. It now reads only who has written in the room, which you had already seen (`src/state/selectors/channel.ts`). A test hides a non-author and asserts silence.

## Accepted trade-offs

- **The address check is a safety net, not a guarantee.** It catches the common shapes in many countries, and it catches invisible characters and non-Latin digits. On the two reviewers' corpora, 35 of 133 and 55 of 138 real addresses still pass (at round 3 it was 54 and 76). Every hotel-room, named-street and via bypass found in round 6 is refused. Airport false alarms: 0 of 22 and 8 of 50. The eight still refused are bare "Room NNN": a conference room reads like a hotel room, and a hotel room is someone's bedroom, so the check refuses it and says why ("Hall 3, Room 305" passes). Deliberate misspellings and look-alike letters get through by design.
- **A pickup note on a self-seating door is readable after one tap.** Circle and link doors seat people without a yes, and taking and leaving are announced in the car's room. Since round 4, coming back after giving the seat up goes to the host.
- **The guardian is a stand-in.** Nothing reaches the named person, because there is no server. The sheet and the chip say so ("Stand-in. Nothing is sent yet…"). The timer and "I'm safe" work on the phone, and a live watch always has an "I'm safe" on Home.

## Deferred (pre-existing, each needs its own design pass)

| Item | Why it is deferred |
|---|---|
| **No backend.** Everything lives on one phone, so a car one classmate offers never reaches another's. | Structural. It is the difference between a demo and the INSEAD cohort actually using it; see the launch memo's server scope. |
| A car's time and door can't change after it is offered, and a called-off car can't be offered again. | A host-only `setRideWindow` through the ride FSM, plus a room line, is the suggested shape. |
| Home doesn't lead with the car you're in on the day. | Wants a "your car today" row. Home now at least skips trips that are over. |
| Groups put people in a room without their yes, and nobody can leave. | Latent until groups sync. It needs the consent model groups never had. |
| Names can be deceptive ("The host", "Wingman"). | Blank and invisible names are closed. Impersonation wants a reserved-word list and a display rule. |
| The matching overlap window can pin an exact window (5.12). | Lives in `src/matching/explain/receipt.ts`, which v9 did not touch. |
| No note on a seat ask (N26); the inbox row time is UTC (N30); the fare-hint spacing (N41); the room's bottom padding (N42). | Minor or nit. |
