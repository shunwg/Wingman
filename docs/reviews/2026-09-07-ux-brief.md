# UX evolution brief — 7 September 2026

Received with a ten-screen reference composition (Welcome · Home · Trip · Person · Chat · Discover · Events · Circles · Profile · In the moment). Condensed; the decisions it forced are in `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-07-v6-premium-ux-design.md`.

**Continuation, not a rebuild.** Preserve routes, functionality, mock data, matching, trips, state, tests. Same product, much better experience.

**Vision.** PERSON × JOURNEY × TIME × PLACE × INTENT. One question: *who should I meet because our paths are about to cross?* A quiet layer over the journey, not a social network.

**Design direction.** Apple-level restraint: simplicity, hierarchy, precision, materiality, warmth, invisible complexity, purposeful animation. Wingman's own identity. Avoid Tinder / LinkedIn / Facebook / generic SaaS.

**Proportions.** iPhone 17 Pro portrait, real safe areas. No vertical stretching, no giant heroes or cards, no presentation-board layouts.

**Visual system.** Warm very-light neutral background; deep navy text; restrained Wingman blue accent; muted grey/green/amber. No neon, gradients, glass, rainbow.

**Type.** Platform/system font. Hierarchy from type, not cards. No marketing type in functional screens.

**Spacing.** 20–24px horizontal, 8px base. Generous, not empty.

**IA.** Simple bottom navigation; profile via avatar; Events and Circles contextual. *(Decision: five tabs kept — Home · Trips · Inbox · Circles · You; Home absorbs Discover.)*

**Home.** A personalised travel dashboard: greeting · next journey · people worth meeting (one person) · upcoming at your destination.

**One person > twenty.** Curated, ranked, focused.

**Matches explain themselves.** Same flight / airport / destination / event / circle / university / interests / time overlap. Explanation over score.

**The moment.** "Your paths cross in Copenhagen. Sofia lands at 12:10. You have 2h 10m together. Say hello → / Not now."

**Person.** Photo · name ✓ · title · school · city · Why meet? · tags · Verified · About · **Say hello**. Never Connect / Follow / Add friend.

**Chat.** Match → conversation → meeting. Surface "Coffee at CPH? Terminal 3 · 12:10–12:50 · Add to calendar · Propose another time".

**Trips.** The primary spatial concept: a timeline with people, events, circles at each stop.

**Discover.** For you · Same flight · Same airport · Same event. Curated cards: who, why relevant, why now. No swipe deck.

**Events.** Contextual to destination, trip, circles, interests.

**Circles.** Trusted communities; simple UI.

**Profile.** Trust, not popularity: trips · people met · events · circles · Verified. No followers, likes, scores.

**Privacy.** First-class. "Same flight · 2h 10m overlap", never gate/seat/live position without consent.

**Notifications.** Rare and useful.

**Animation.** Subtle, relational. No bouncing, parallax, confetti.

**Process.** Audit → design system → core (Home, Person, Chat, Meeting) → Trips → Discover → Events + Circles → Profile + Privacy → Polish. Self-critique every screen at device size. Final test: *I tell Wingman where I'm going. It understands my journey. It finds the people. It tells me why. I say hello. We meet.*
