What was built, and what is left to go live

Two days took a seeded demo to a product a stranger can register on. This is the record of that, and the full list of what stands between the current build and real people using it.

6 September 2026 for Shun Gong, Tide Capital build 05a1871 · main shunwg.github.io/Wingman

In three sentences

Wingman now has doors (welcome, sign-up, BankID-first verification, a first trip), a commercial unit (circles with admission by email ending or pasted list, marks, badges, an organiser dashboard, a QR invite, and a board for members with no flight), one inbox for every conversation with hide, report, mute and a guardian on every surface, and a matching engine that ranks on conduct and complementarity and never on looks. It scored 9.55 with a Jobs-style scorecard and 9.5 with a Thiel-style one, and every gate is green in CI. Everything still lives on the person's own device: the work left is a backend, real logins, real identity providers, a domain, the legal paperwork, and the native shell for the App Store.

What was done, 5–6 September

Eleven work streams, each merged to main only after typecheck, the purity gate, the import-boundary check, all tests and the end-to-end flows passed. Commits are on GitHub; the plans are under docs/superpowers/plans/.

What "live" means

Three thresholds, in order. Each is a real product for a real audience; none requires the next.

A · Web beta

A real domain, real accounts, data on a server, one pilot circle. No app store. Reachable in roughly eight weeks of focused work. This is where the first organiser sale happens.

B · TestFlight

The same app inside a native shell, on invited iPhones. Push notifications become possible. Adds three to four weeks on top of A, and a Mac.

C · App Store

Public listing. Apple's review is the long pole: moderation commitments, Sign in with Apple, account deletion, age rating. Budget four weeks of review cycles after B.

Everything left, by area

Chips say who does the work. built exists in the code behind a seam and needs wiring; work is engineering; dependency is someone else's account, approval or invoice. Effort is one engineer, honest.

1 · Accounts and login

Today an account is a device. The seam is account.provider: it reads 'device' and becomes 'supabase'; nothing else in the app switches on it.

Supabase project and Auth
work · 2 days · Supabase Pro $25/mo

Create the project in the EU region (Frankfurt). Turn on email magic-link and OTP. This one provider also powers work-email verification and circle admission, so it is the first login to ship.

Sign in with Apple
work · 1 day · Apple Developer $99/yr

Mandatory the moment Google or any third-party login is offered (App Store guideline 4.8), and the cheapest way to satisfy it. Steps: enrol in the Developer Program (a company enrolment needs a D-U-N-S number, allow two weeks; a personal one is same-day), create an App ID with the Sign in with Apple capability, a Services ID for the web, and a private key; paste the three into Supabase's Apple provider. Apple hides the user's email behind a relay unless they opt out; the app already treats email as a verification input, not an identity, so this is fine.

Google login
work · half a day · Google Cloud, free

A Google Cloud project, an OAuth consent screen (external, with the privacy policy URL, so the domain and the policy come first), and a web client ID with Supabase's callback URL. The same client serves the Google stamp, so do this once and reuse it.

Account deletion and export
work · 1 day

One Edge Function that cascades the delete, revokes the session and removes guardian sessions and location pings. Required by App Store 5.1.1(v) and by GDPR; the "Sign out and start over" button already exists as the place it goes.

2 · Data on a server

Schema and row-level security
work · 2 weeks

Tables for persons, trips, requests, meets, messages, channels, circles, memberships, stamps, reports, ratings, guardian sessions. RLS policies mirror the two-rule privacy model that today runs in the browser: a row is readable only when both audience rules pass in both directions. The engines are pure TypeScript with no DOM dependency, so the same code runs in an Edge Function unchanged; the browser copy stays as the instant preview.

Server-side matching and the board
work · 1 week · engine

Candidates are computed on read, never stored (rule 2). One function: take the viewer, the mutually visible pool, return redacted cards. The stable pick then runs over the real pool rather than one viewer's list.

Realtime inbox
work · 3 days

Supabase Realtime on messages and requests. The inbox already derives rows from state, so it is a subscription, not a rewrite. Scripted demo replies are removed at the same time.

Migration of the local store
work · 2 days · migrate chain

Version 5 of the persisted store: a device account becomes a server account on first login, uploading trips and profile once. Nobody who used the beta loses a trip.

Photo uploads
work · 1 day

Supabase Storage bucket, 256 px square, already resized client-side. Uploads bring image moderation into scope (see Safety).

3 · Identity stamps

Every provider is already mocked behind one contract in src/stamps/providers/; stamps/registry.ts selects mock or real on an environment variable. Each real one is a file and a registry line. The time goes to their approval processes.

Work email (OTP)
work · 1 day · email-domain.ts

Supabase Auth OTP to the address; the domain of a verified address becomes the stamp. Cheapest and most valuable: it is the gate for circle admission and therefore for the whole school and conference motion. Ship first.

BankID (Norway) via Criipto
work · 3 days · Criipto ~€99/mo + €0.35/verification, contract, 1–2 weeks

Nobody integrates BankID directly; a broker does. Criipto is self-serve with a test environment; Signicat is enterprise and quoted. Needs a Norwegian organisation number and a signed agreement. The deep-link return trip into the app is the fiddly part on iOS. The stamp is charged once per person, so it stays opt-in.

LinkedIn
work · 1 day · LinkedIn Developer app, approval days to weeks

"Sign In with LinkedIn using OpenID Connect" gives name, photo and email; the profile URL for the contact card needs the app to be associated with a company page. Create the app, request the product, wait.

Google
work · half a day

Same client as the login; a stamp is just a verified Google identity.

Instagram and Facebook
work · 2 days · Meta app review, 2–6 weeks, slowest of all

Meta requires a business verification and an app review with a screencast. Recommendation: leave both out of the first release and keep the mocked rows hidden; the trust story stands on BankID, work email and LinkedIn.

4 · Flights and places

Live flight schedules
work · 2 days · AeroDataBox or FlightAware AeroAPI, ~$10–220/mo by volume

An Edge Function holds the key and proxies the provider; the client's degrade chain (live → cache → stale → bundled → synthetic → manual) is built and does not change shape. Without a key the app already works worldwide from the bundled table.

Guardian delivery
work · 1 day · Twilio, pennies

The "tell someone where you are" link is minted today; sending it as an SMS or email is a Twilio or Resend call from an Edge Function. The capability URL dies when the session ends, as designed.

5 · Domain, hosting, notifications

A domain
decision · 1 hour · registrar, ~$20–60/yr

Needed before the OAuth consent screens, the privacy policy and the App Store listing can be filled in. Everything today is served under /Wingman/ on GitHub Pages; hash routing means the move is a DNS change and one build flag.

Vercel and environments
work · half a day · Vercel Pro $20/mo

Production and a preview per branch. Secrets live in Vercel and Supabase, never in VITE_* variables, which ship to the browser.

Transactional email
work · half a day · Resend or Postmark, free tier

OTP codes, circle invitations, "someone said yes". Custom domain with SPF and DKIM so the codes do not land in spam.

Push notifications
work · 3 days · needs the native shell

Web push covers Android and desktop browsers; iOS push needs the app installed from the store or TestFlight. The in-app activity dot is the honest substitute until then.

6 · Legal and compliance

Privacy policy and terms
work · 2 days, a lawyer's afternoon

Location, identity documents and a social graph make this a high-risk processing activity under GDPR; a Data Protection Impact Assessment is expected, and the two-rule privacy model plus bucketed counts are exactly the mitigations it wants to see written down. Datatilsynet is the supervisory authority. Both documents are required by the OAuth consent screens and the App Store before anything else can proceed.

Data processing agreements
practical · 1 day

Sign the standard DPAs with Supabase, Vercel, Criipto, the flight-data provider and the email provider. Keep the list in the policy's sub-processor section.

Age gate and store rating
work · half a day

18+. A checkbox at sign-up, and the 17+ rating on the listing. No date of birth is stored; the stamps contract forbids it.

Apple Privacy Nutrition Labels and the review notes
practical · 1 day

List precise location (guardian), identifiers, contacts, photos, and user content. Provide a demo account and the #/demo path in the review notes. Screenshots and description must lead with the social and professional use; the first-run screen already says what the app is.

7 · Safety operations

Moderation queue and a named person
practical · ongoing · reports

Reports are already written with reason, note and target. On a server they become a queue. Apple's guideline 1.2 requires a timely response by a person; at pilot scale that is minutes a day, but someone has to own it before it is promised in the listing.

Image moderation
work · 1 day · Hive or AWS Rekognition, ~$50/mo at 10k MAU

Runs on every uploaded photo before it is visible. Same queue as the reports.

Abuse runbook
practical · half a day

What happens on a report with a safety flag: suspend, notify, preserve evidence, when to involve police. Write it before the first real report, not after.

8 · The native shell

Capacitor, iOS and Android
work · 1 week · a Mac or a macOS CI runner; Google Play $25 once

The web app inside a native shell. Purpose strings for location and camera, deep links for the BankID return trip, universal links so invitations open the app. No Windows path exists to an iOS build: buy a Mac or rent a runner (GitHub-hosted macOS or Codemagic) in week one.

TestFlight, then review
practical · 3–6 weeks of cycles

Certificates, an App Store Connect record, the listing, the labels, and the first submission. Expect one rejection; the usual ones are 1.2 (moderation), 4.8 (Sign in with Apple), 5.1.1 (deletion). All three are addressed above.

9 · Commercial

One pilot circle
practical · now

A school or a conference with a date. The organiser's ten minutes (create, paste the list, QR, pin a welcome, open a track) are built and tested end to end; what is missing is a real room to run them in. This is the single most useful thing to do before any engineering above.

Pricing and invoicing
work · 2 days · Stripe

Per event for conferences, per seat per year for schools; travellers free; no ads. Stripe Checkout on the organiser page; the "Free while in beta" chip on the invite screen comes off.

Sponsor report export
work · 1 day · bucketed counts

A PDF of the admin screen's numbers, bucketed as they are on screen. Never an exact figure on a small circle.

10 · Operations

Error tracking, analytics, uptime, backups
work · 1 day · Sentry, Plausible, free tiers

Privacy-preserving analytics only (no cookies, no third-party identifiers); the local, bucketed event counts already in the app are the model. Point-in-time recovery on the database. A staging project that mirrors production.

RLS load test
work · 1 day

The policy runs per row on every read. Fine at pilot scale; the first thing that breaks at ten thousand people. Test it while the schema is cheap to change.

The order

Week 0
Decisions and accounts

Domain. Apple Developer enrolment (company needs D-U-N-S: start now). Google Cloud project. Supabase project. Criipto test account. LinkedIn Developer app (start the approval). A pilot organiser with a date. Who moderates.

Weeks 1–2
Accounts

Supabase Auth with email OTP, Sign in with Apple, Google. Privacy policy and terms drafted, because the consent screens need their URL.

Weeks 3–5
Data

Schema, RLS, the store migration, server-side matching, realtime inbox, photo uploads, image moderation, account deletion.

Weeks 6–7
Stamps and flights

Work email live. LinkedIn and Google stamps. BankID in Criipto's test environment. Flight-data proxy. Guardian delivery.

Week 8
Web beta · threshold A

Real domain, one pilot circle, moderation owned. Sell the second circle.

Weeks 9–12
Native shell · threshold B

Capacitor, deep links, push, TestFlight with the pilot's delegates.

Weeks 13–16
App Store · threshold C

Listing, labels, review notes, submission, one rejection, resubmission. BankID switched to production when a customer asks for it by name.

Decisions only you can make

  • The domain name. Everything downstream is filled in with it.
  • Apple enrolment as a person or as Tide Capital / a new company. Company needs a D-U-N-S number and two weeks; it is also the name on the listing.
  • Meta stamps in the first release, or not. Recommendation: not.
  • When BankID goes to production. It is more than half the monthly bill and nothing needs it until an organiser asks.
  • Who moderates. A named person, before the listing promises a timely response.
  • The first pilot. One school or one conference, with a date on the calendar.

What it costs

From the deployment memo's calculator, unchanged: at pilot scale roughly $176 a month all in, $63 without BankID; at 10,000 monthly actives about $289; at 100,000 about $1,285. One-off: Apple $99 a year, Google Play $25, a domain, a Mac or a CI runner, and a lawyer's afternoon. The engineering above is roughly twelve to sixteen weeks for one person who knows the codebase.
