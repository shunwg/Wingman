# Persona: Reluctant host — Sara, 38

## Snapshot
- Age: 38
- Tech savviness (1–5): 3. Competent, not curious. Uses what her employer installs and turns off what she can find.
- Flying habits: Oslo–Amsterdam and Oslo–London for work, six or seven times a year, usually landing at night. Takes a cab into town alone and has always slightly hated it.
- Device: iPhone 13, Do Not Disturb permanently on.

## Goals
Sharing a car into town at 23:00 would genuinely be better than doing it alone — cheaper, and there is somebody there. She is willing to be the one who offers. But she will offer on her terms: she decides who is in the car, she wants to know something real about them first, and she wants a way out if it feels wrong.

## Fears and friction
- Being in a moving car with someone she cannot get away from. This is the whole thing. A meet in a café she can leave; a cab she cannot.
- A control that sounds like a promise but is not one. "Women only" had better mean it, and she will look for evidence rather than take the word.
- Being seated with somebody automatically, before she has agreed. If the app decides for her, she will not use the app.
- Her exact whereabouts becoming visible because she offered a car. She is being helpful, not publishing an itinerary.
- Awkwardness afterwards: a stranger with her number, a stranger who now knows where she stays.

## Privacy stance (1–5)
4. Not technical about it — situational. She thinks about who is physically going to be next to her and works backwards from that.

## Voice when annoyed
"Who exactly can see this?" · "I didn't agree to that." · "It put him in the car." · "Can I undo it?" · "This feels like it's for people braver than me."

## Success criteria
- Nobody enters her car without her explicit yes, and the screen makes clear beforehand which behaviour she is choosing.
- She can restrict the car to women, or to a circle, and then verify from the screen that the restriction is real.
- She can see something about a person before saying yes — more than a first name if she is going to sit beside them.
- She can call the car off, and the screen says what happens to whoever was in it.
- Offering a car does not reveal where she is staying or her exact movements.
- She can take back a seat she already granted, and it is not buried.
