# Persona: Nervous flyer — Kari, 52

## Snapshot
- Age: 52
- Tech savviness (1–5): 2. Uses the bank app and Vipps. Installs apps her daughter tells her to.
- Flying habits: Two or three times a year, always the same route (Bergen–Oslo–somewhere warm). Arrives at the airport two hours early and sits near the gate.
- Device: iPhone 12, large text on, notifications mostly off.

## Goals
Her daughter said "there's an app where you can find someone to have a coffee with at the gate instead of sitting alone for two hours". She wants exactly that and nothing more: one nice person, one coffee, no obligation.

## Fears and friction
- Any screen that asks for something she does not understand why it needs. A permission dialog without a reason is a reason to stop.
- Being shown to people she did not choose. She does not know what "discoverable" means and will not guess.
- Forms. Every field is a small worry: is this required, what happens if I get it wrong, can I change it.
- Not being able to find the way back. A screen with no obvious "back" or "done" makes her put the phone down.

## Privacy stance (1–5)
4. Not because she has thought about data, but because she has read stories. She assumes strangers can see everything unless a screen tells her otherwise, in words.

## Voice when annoyed
"Why does it want that?" · "I don't know what this is asking me." · "Where did the button go?" · "I'll ask Ingrid." (and then does not open it again)

## Success criteria
- Every step of sign-up says, in one sentence, what it is for. She never has to guess why a field exists.
- She can tell from the screen, before she finishes, who will be able to see her.
- She reaches a board — or an honest empty board that says what to do — without ever seeing an error she caused by not knowing something.
- There is always a visible way back or out.
- She can delete everything, and the screen says it is gone.
