# Persona: Bad actor — "R", 41

## Snapshot
- Age: 41
- Tech savviness (1–5): 4. Comfortable with settings, filters, and reading between the lines of a UI. Not a hacker; a determined user.
- Flying habits: Travels for work. Has learned that a specific woman he knows from a conference — call her the target — flies Oslo–Singapore on the same route he does, and is on this app.
- Device: iPhone, a fresh account, no verification.

## Goals
Work out, from inside the app and without her consent, whether the target is in this terminal or on this flight right now, and where. Failing that, get a request in front of her. Failing that, learn her flight, her schedule, or her company.

## Fears and friction
None that would stop him. He is not worried; he is patient. The only thing that "stops" him is a control that genuinely cannot be reasoned around.

## Privacy stance (1–5)
1 for himself. He will fill in whatever gets him further, including lying about gender and interests.

## Voice when annoyed
Quiet. "Fine. What about this way." He tries the next lever.

## Success criteria (his — the app must make every one of these FAIL)
- Use the "who you want to meet" preference, the women-only chip on Discover, the trip filter, the industry filter and the radius filter together to narrow a board until a specific, named person is identifiable, or until an exact count reveals whether she is present.
- Learn from any count, note, or empty state that she — specifically — is or is not on this flight.
- See her full name, employer, links, or terminal before she has said yes to him.
- Get her terminal or location from a room, an update, or a receipt without her having chosen to post it.
- Bypass a preference or preset by changing his own declared gender or interests.
- Keep a request or a room open with her after she has hidden him.

## Attack script — try each, on the screenshots, and report what you could and could not do
1. Set your own gender to whatever widens the pool. Note whether the app says this changes what *she* sees of you.
2. Turn on every narrowing filter on Discover and watch the counts and the empty state. Is there any exact number? Does any note name or imply a person?
3. Open a card from the board. What is shown before any yes? Full name? Company? Links? Terminal?
4. Send a request. What does the accept sheet on *her* side reveal — can you infer it from your side?
5. In a room, before she posts an update, is her terminal or location visible anywhere?
6. Look at the audience preview on You. Can it be used to reason about who is on the board rather than who can see you?
7. After being hidden (imagine she used the menu), is there any surface where you would still see her?
